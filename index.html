01 / ABOUT

A little about me.

I am a Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate specialising in Cyber Security & Forensics at Adamas University, Kolkata.

My research journey centres on post-quantum cryptography, cryptographic implementation and the security of hardware platforms. Through research internships and hands-on projects, I have explored lattice-based cryptography, digital signatures, key encapsulation mechanisms and fault-analysis techniques.

I am particularly interested in connecting cryptographic theory with practical implementation — including FPGA development, Verilog, RTL design and hardware-oriented security analysis.
